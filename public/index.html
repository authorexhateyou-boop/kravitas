FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a token?

Tokens pay for what the AI makes. A Motion ad (animated photo with voice-over and captions) is about 9 tokens, a Lite talking ad with real AI video about 32, and an image 6 to 16. You always see the cost on the Generate button before you click.

Do I need to show my face?

No. Pick a ready-made AI creator from the avatar library, or make a showcase ad that focuses on the product.

Does it work in Bahasa Melayu?

Yes. Scripts, captions and voice-overs can be in Bahasa Melayu, English or Manglish.

Do I need to sign up?

Not to try it. Opening the studio gives you a guest account with 40 free tokens in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes that guest account.

Can I edit the script before it renders?

Yes. With Approve first switched on, Kravitas drafts the hook, script and keyframe for a few tokens, and only makes the voice and video after you approve.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Monthly plans are paid by card through Stripe. One-off top-ups also accept FPX online banking and GrabPay. Cancel anytime from your account.
